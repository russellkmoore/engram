<objective>
PRE-02 installs the testing discipline for v0.2. Three goals are coupled by design:
(1) Tier separation — `unit` runs offline without CF creds; `integration` and `eval` require creds (eval tier is what runs against real Workers AI + Vectorize and produces the corpus metrics PRE-03's labeling supports).
(2) Cost ceiling — `MAX_AI_CALLS=200` is the literal contract for a single eval run. Without it, a forgotten describe.only or a runaway loop bills neurons forever (PITFALLS INT-2). The ceiling is enforced from inside vitest (counter throws at 201) AND verified after the fact (Analytics Engine GraphQL sum).
(3) CI fork-safety — fork PRs cannot access CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN, but a careless workflow would still attempt the eval job and produce a confusing skip. Gate the job explicitly: `github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name == github.repository`.

The vitest tier shape mirrors the existing multi-project configs at `packages/mcp-server/vitest.config.ts:35-100` and `packages/workspace-do/vitest.config.ts:44-75` — this plan extends that shape, it does not invent a new one.
</objective>
